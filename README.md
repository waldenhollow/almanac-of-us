# Almanac of Us

A heartfelt milestone calendar of your life events you don't know about.

It is a free page that finds the anniversaries a family does not know it has.

The lead example is the **Echo Day**: the one day you are exactly the age your parent was when you were born. On that day they are exactly twice your age. Put in two birthdays and the page finds it, along with every other day like it: 10,000 days alive, the day you have known someone for half your life, the day two ages add up to 100, planet birthdays, and the day the dog overtakes you in dog years.

## Use it

Open it here: https://waldenhollow.github.io/almanac-of-us/ (once GitHub Pages is switched on), or download `index.html` and open it in any browser.

- Free. No account, no ads, no payment.
- Nothing you type leaves your browser. There is no server.
- You can save any day as a card, download a calendar file, print the year, or make a link to send. That link carries the names and birthdays inside it, so share it only with the people it is for.

## How it works

It is arithmetic on dates. `source/core.js` is the whole engine and has no dependencies. `index.html` is that engine plus the page, in one file you can host anywhere.
